## What's in the pack

JAMstack — JavaScript, APIs, and Markup — has largely become shorthand for "statically-built or edge-rendered frontend deployed to a CDN." The modern incarnation looks like Astro, Next.js, or SvelteKit on the build side, with Vercel or Netlify on the host side. These seven icons cover the frameworks and hosts you'll reach for most when illustrating a JAMstack architecture.

## How to use them

For a canonical JAMstack diagram: put the framework icons (Astro, Next.js, Nuxt, SvelteKit, Gatsby) on the build side, the hosts (Vercel, Netlify) on the deploy side, and arrows pointing out to a CDN edge. Nuxt and SvelteKit both have tall marks — give them matching vertical bounds so the row reads evenly. Avoid placing Gatsby too prominently unless it's specifically relevant; the framework has lost share to Astro and Next.js for most new projects.
