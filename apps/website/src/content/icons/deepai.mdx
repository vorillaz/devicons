## About the DeepAI logo

The DeepAI logo is a leaping dolphin whose body curls into a circle around a cresting wave, with a small square dot to its left. On deepai.org it appears without a wordmark. This file is the vector logo from the deepai.org site header. DeepAI says it started in late 2016 with the first browser-based text-to-image generator.

The site draws the logo in dark slate (#33314A) on its light theme and in white on its dark theme. The colored file uses the dark slate, so use the monochrome version on dark backgrounds.

## When to use this logo

Use the DeepAI logo in AI image generator comparisons, lists of free AI chat and creative tools, and docs or tutorials that call DeepAI's APIs for text-to-image, super resolution or background removal. It pairs naturally with Midjourney, Leonardo AI, Craiyon, Stable Diffusion and ChatGPT in creative AI tool roundups.
