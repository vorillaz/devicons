## About the Directus logo

The Directus icon is a white rabbit in mid-leap on a square tile of layered purple waves, which shade from Directus purple (#6644FF) to lighter violet (#B36EEE). Directus also uses the rabbit on its own, in white, in its site header.

The purple tile reads well on light and dark backgrounds.

## When to use this logo

Use Directus in headless CMS comparisons, Jamstack and backend-as-a-service stack diagrams, self-hosting guides and docs for the Directus SDK, REST or GraphQL APIs. It pairs naturally with Strapi, Payload, PostgreSQL, Nuxt and Next.js.
