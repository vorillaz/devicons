## About the Kling AI logo

The current Kling AI mark is an outlined shape: two curved, interlocking bands that wrap around an inner ellipse, drawn with a single line weight. The full logo pairs it with a geometric "KlingAI" wordmark. Both files come from the vector logo in the header of kling.ai.

The logo is monochrome. Kling uses it in white on its dark website, and this set ships it in black (#000000). Use the monochrome version, which follows the text color, on dark backgrounds.

## When to use this logo

Use the Kling AI logo in AI video generator comparisons, text-to-video and image-to-video model pickers, creative tool directories, and docs for APIs or workflow tools that call Kling models. It pairs naturally with Runway, Pika, Luma Dream Machine, Hailuo, PixVerse and Google Veo, and with Kuaishou when you show the company behind it.
