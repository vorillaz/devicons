## About the OBS Studio logo

The OBS logo is a dark charcoal (#302E31) circle with three light grey (#C4C2C4) comma-shaped swirls turning around the centre, like a camera aperture. A thin white ring around the circle separates it from dark backgrounds.

The logo reads well on light and dark backgrounds.

## When to use this logo

Use OBS Studio in streaming setup guides, screen recording tool comparisons, creator stack slides and OBS plugin docs. It pairs naturally with Twitch, YouTube, Discord and Remotion.
