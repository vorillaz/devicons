## About the Google Maps logo

The Google Maps logo is a location pin split into Google's four colours: blue (#4285F4 and #1A73E8), red (#EA4335), yellow (#FBBC04) and green (#34A853), with a round hole near the top. Google introduced the pin in February 2020 for the service's 15th anniversary. It replaced the folded paper map icon.

The pin reads well on light and dark backgrounds.

## When to use this logo

Use Google Maps in store locators, delivery and travel app designs, "get directions" buttons and docs for the Maps JavaScript API, Places API or Routes API. It also fits geospatial stack diagrams and mapping library comparisons. It pairs naturally with Google Cloud, Mapbox, Leaflet, OpenStreetMap and Uber.
