## About the Canva logo

The Canva icon is a circle filled with a diagonal gradient from teal (#00C4CC) to violet (#7D2AE7), with a white script "C" in the middle. The cursive letter comes from the Canva wordmark, and the gradient circle is the mark Canva uses for its app icon and favicon.

The gradient circle reads well on light and dark backgrounds.

## When to use this logo

Use Canva in design tool comparisons, marketing stack diagrams, social media workflow guides and docs for the Canva Apps SDK or Connect APIs. It also fits "edit in Canva" buttons and template marketplaces. It pairs naturally with Figma, Adobe Express, Photoshop, Google Slides and PowerPoint.
