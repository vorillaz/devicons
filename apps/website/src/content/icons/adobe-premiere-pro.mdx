## About the Adobe Premiere Pro logo

The Premiere Pro icon is a dark navy rounded square (#00005B) with the letters "Pr" in lavender (#9999FF). It follows Adobe's app icon system of a two-letter mnemonic on a dark tile, and shares its colour pair with After Effects and Animate.

The navy tile blends into very dark backgrounds, but the lavender letters stay legible.

## When to use this logo

Use Adobe Premiere Pro in video editing software comparisons, post-production workflow diagrams, .prproj file-type icons and docs for Premiere Pro extensions. It pairs naturally with Adobe After Effects, Adobe Photoshop, Remotion, OBS Studio and YouTube.
