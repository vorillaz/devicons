## About the Civitai logo

The Civitai mark is a hexagon with a bright blue outer ring, a dark navy center and a white, angular "C". The wordmark spells "CIVITAI" in blocky letters, with a small notch cut into the "V". On civitai.com the mark shows on small screens and the wordmark on larger ones. Both files use the site's default colors; the site swaps in seasonal colors for holidays such as Halloween.

The ring fades from blue (#1284F7) to deep blue (#0A20C9). The wordmark is dark grey (#222222) with "AI" in blue (#1971C2). The dark grey letters are hard to see on dark backgrounds, so use the monochrome version in dark themes.

## When to use this logo

Use the Civitai logo in model hub comparisons, Stable Diffusion and Flux workflow docs, checkpoint and LoRA download guides, and lists of generative art tools. The hexagon mark fits compact spots such as model source badges, provider pickers and app sidebars, while the wordmark suits headers and partner sections. It pairs naturally with Hugging Face, ComfyUI, AUTOMATIC1111, Stable Diffusion and Flux.
