## About the iFLYTEK Spark logo

The iFLYTEK Spark mark is a water drop with a flame curling out of its top. The drop is light blue (#3DC8F9), the flame tip is red (#EA0100), and a darker blue band (#1652D8) wraps around the lower right. The full logo sets the mark next to the Chinese name 讯飞星火 with "iFLYTEK SPARK" spaced out underneath. The layout and lettering come from the lockup on xinghuo.xfyun.cn, and the colored mark matches the site's favicon.

The wordmark is black, so it disappears on dark backgrounds. In dark themes, use the monochrome version or the mark on its own.

## When to use this logo

Use the iFLYTEK Spark logo in LLM provider pickers, model comparison tables, Chinese AI model benchmarks and docs for SDKs or gateways that call the Spark API. It pairs naturally with ERNIE, Qwen, Doubao, GLM, Kimi and DeepSeek, and with iFLYTEK when you show the company behind the model. The mark alone fits small spaces such as model badges and chat avatars.
