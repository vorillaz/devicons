## About the Proton VPN logo

The Proton VPN icon is a rounded, downward-pointing triangle filled with gradients from purple (#6D4AFF, #7341FF) to aqua (#00F0C3, #8EFFEE). It arrived with Proton's 2022 rebrand, which gave all of Proton's apps a shared purple identity. The lockup adds the wordmark with "Proton" in dark navy (#1B1340) and "VPN" in purple.

The icon reads well on light and dark backgrounds. The navy "Proton" disappears on dark surfaces, so use the monochrome lockup there.

## When to use this logo

Use Proton VPN in VPN comparisons, privacy tool stacks and network security guides. It pairs naturally with Proton Mail, WireGuard, Tailscale and Bitwarden.
