## About the Google Classroom logo

The Google Classroom icon is a green chalkboard (#0F9D58) in a yellow frame (#F4B400). Three figures sit in the middle, a white one in front and two light green ones (#57BB8A) behind, and a short white line in the bottom-right corner looks like chalk on the board's ledge.

The icon reads well on light and dark backgrounds.

## When to use this logo

Use Google Classroom in edtech tool comparisons, LMS integration docs, school IT setup guides and docs for the Google Classroom API. It pairs naturally with Google Meet, Google Drive, Google Slides, Moodle and Microsoft Teams.
