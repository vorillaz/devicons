## About the PixVerse logo

The PixVerse mark is a stylized "P" made of two overlapping, translucent shapes: a narrow vertical strip and a rounded, kite-like bowl that folds over it. The full logo pairs the mark with a bold, rounded "PixVerse" wordmark. Both come from the vector logo in the header of pixverse.ai.

The mark uses gradients that run from orange (#FE9E58) through pink-violet (#E470EC) to deep indigo (#442FBA). PixVerse shows the wordmark in light grey on its dark site. This set uses a black wordmark, so in dark themes use the monochrome version or the mark on its own. The monochrome mark keeps the fold as thin gaps.

## When to use this logo

Use the PixVerse logo in AI video generator comparisons, text-to-video and image-to-video model pickers, creator tool directories, and docs for APIs or automation workflows that call PixVerse. It pairs naturally with Kling AI, Runway, Pika, Luma Dream Machine, Hailuo and Google Veo. The mark alone works well in compact spots such as model badges, generation history lists and app launchers.
