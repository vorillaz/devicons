## About the CapCut logo

CapCut's mark is a geometric glyph: a rounded rectangle crossed by two diagonal bands that meet in the middle, forming an hourglass-like "cut" shape with a small tab extending to the right. On capcut.com the glyph sits to the left of the "CapCut" wordmark, set in a rounded geometric sans with the two capital Cs echoing each other.

The logo is single-color. capcut.com renders it in black on light surfaces, and the brand has no secondary accent color in the mark itself. The colored file here is black (#000000), so on dark interfaces use the mono version, which takes `currentColor`.

## When to use this logo

Use the CapCut logo in creator-tool roundups, short-form video workflow diagrams, app comparison tables and tutorials on editing for social platforms. Its natural neighbors are TikTok (also from ByteDance), Adobe Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve and Canva. The standalone glyph works well as a small app badge; switch to the full lockup when the name needs to be read.
