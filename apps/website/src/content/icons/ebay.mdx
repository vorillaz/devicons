## About the eBay logo

The eBay logo is the lowercase wordmark "ebay" with each letter in its own colour: a red (#E53238) "e", a blue (#0064D2) "b", a yellow (#F5AF02) "a" and a green (#86B817) "y". The letters overlap slightly. eBay simplified the wordmark in 2012 with thinner, evenly set letters and kept the four colours of the earlier logo.

The coloured letters read well on light and dark backgrounds.

## When to use this logo

Use eBay in marketplace integration docs, multichannel selling tools, e-commerce platform comparisons and "buy on eBay" links. It pairs naturally with Amazon, Etsy, Shopify, AliExpress and PayPal.
