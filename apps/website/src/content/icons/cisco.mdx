## About the Cisco logo

The Cisco logo stacks a row of vertical bars of different heights over the lowercase "cisco" wordmark, all in cyan blue (#00BCEB). The bars trace the outline of a suspension bridge, usually read as the Golden Gate Bridge, a nod to the company's San Francisco roots and its name.

The cyan logo reads well on light and dark backgrounds.

## When to use this logo

Use Cisco in network architecture diagrams, enterprise IT stack slides, certification study guides and integration docs for Cisco APIs. It pairs naturally with Juniper Networks, Fortinet, Palo Alto Networks, Splunk and Webex.
