## About the Binance logo

The Binance logo mark is a gold-yellow (#F3BA2F) diamond pattern: a central square turned 45 degrees, four L-shaped chevrons around it and two small diamonds at the sides. Together the pieces form a larger diamond.

The yellow mark reads well on light and dark backgrounds.

## When to use this logo

Use Binance in crypto exchange comparisons, payment and wallet integrations, trading bot docs for the Binance API and fintech dashboards. It pairs naturally with Coinbase, Tether, Bitcoin, Ethereum and MetaMask.
