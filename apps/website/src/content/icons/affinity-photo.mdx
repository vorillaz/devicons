## About the Affinity Photo logo

The Affinity Photo icon is a dark purple rounded square (#4E3188) with a geometric triangular mark made of overlapping pink and magenta facets (#F9A6FF to #EA73FF). It is the version 2 icon, which shares its faceted triangle with Affinity Designer and Affinity Publisher in different colours.

Canva acquired Serif in 2024. In October 2025 it merged Photo, Designer and Publisher into a single free Affinity app.

The icon reads well on light and dark backgrounds.

## When to use this logo

Use Affinity Photo in photo editing software comparisons, Photoshop alternative roundups, creative workflow diagrams and .afphoto file-type icons. It pairs naturally with Adobe Photoshop, GIMP, Canva, Adobe Illustrator and Figma.
