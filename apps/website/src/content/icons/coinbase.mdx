## About the Coinbase logo

The Coinbase icon is a blue (#0052FF) circle with a white "C" whose opening holds a small blue square. The wordmark is the lowercase word "coinbase" in black.

The blue icon reads well on light and dark backgrounds. The black wordmark disappears on dark surfaces, so use the monochrome version in white there.

## When to use this logo

Use Coinbase in crypto exchange comparisons, "connect wallet" and crypto payment buttons, onchain app docs and fintech dashboards. It pairs naturally with Binance, MetaMask, Ethereum, Solana and Stripe.
