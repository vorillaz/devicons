## About the Kimi logo

The Kimi logomark is a rounded black square holding a geometric white "K" with a small blue (#1783FF) droplet at its upper right. The main lockup sets the mark next to an uppercase "KIMI" wordmark in black. Both files come from the logo assets on the official Kimi Brand Book, which also names a signature brand blue as the center of its color system.

The black tile and black wordmark fade into dark backgrounds. The brand book ships a white-tile version for dark themes, and the monochrome files here work in either theme.

## When to use this logo

Use the Kimi logo in LLM provider pickers, model comparison tables, benchmark charts and open-weight model catalogs that list Kimi K2 or K3. It also fits docs for inference servers, coding agents and SDK integrations that call the Kimi API. It pairs naturally with Moonshot AI, DeepSeek, Qwen, GLM and MiniMax. Use the logomark alone for avatars and small sizes.
