## About the Tabby logo

The Tabby logo is a 3D chevron, a right-pointing ">" like a shell prompt, shaded in pale blue and teal gradients (#CCECFF, #77DBDB, #669ABD). The app was called Terminus before it was renamed Tabby.

The pale logo reads well on dark backgrounds and holds up on light ones thanks to its darker shading.

## When to use this logo

Use Tabby in terminal emulator comparisons, SSH client roundups and developer setup guides. It pairs naturally with Warp, Ghostty, Alacritty, iTerm2 and Windows Terminal.
