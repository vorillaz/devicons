## About the vLLM logo

The vLLM mark is a stylized lowercase "v" made of two flat shapes: a yellow #FDB515 triangle on the left and a taller, slanted blue #30A2FF stroke on the right. In the full logo, the mark sits before "LLM" in a thin, geometric sans-serif set in dark gray #434343. The project's website publishes the full logo in light and dark-mode versions.

The two-color mark works on light and dark backgrounds. The gray wordmark has low contrast on dark backgrounds, where the site uses a near-white #F3F3F3 version. On dark backgrounds, use the monochrome version.

## When to use this logo

Use vLLM in LLM serving and inference architecture diagrams, self-hosting and GPU deployment guides, throughput benchmark tables, and docs for OpenAI-compatible inference endpoints. It also fits model card "serve with" sections. It pairs naturally with Hugging Face, PyTorch, NVIDIA, Ray, Kubernetes and inference alternatives such as Ollama, llama.cpp and SGLang.
