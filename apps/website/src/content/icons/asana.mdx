## About the Asana logo

The Asana icon is three coral (#F06A6A) dots arranged in a triangle, one on top and two below. The lockup adds "asana" in a lowercase sans serif in near-black (#0D0E10).

The coral dots read well on light and dark backgrounds. The dark wordmark disappears on dark surfaces, so use the icon or the monochrome lockup there.

## When to use this logo

Use Asana in project management tool comparisons, team productivity stacks, workflow automation diagrams and docs for Asana integrations or the Asana API. It pairs naturally with Jira, Trello, Notion, Slack and Linear.
