## About the Google Meet logo

The Google Meet icon is a video camera built from coloured blocks: blue (#0066DA, #2684FC), red (#E94235), yellow and green (#00AC47, #00832D), with a square cut-out in the camera body and a green lens on the right. Google introduced it in October 2020, when G Suite became Google Workspace and its apps got matching four-colour icons.

The icon reads well on light and dark backgrounds.

## When to use this logo

Use Google Meet in video conferencing comparisons, "join with Google Meet" buttons, scheduling integrations and remote work stack slides. It pairs naturally with Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Calendar and Slack.
