## About the Lovart logo

The Lovart mark is a solid circle with three cut-out shapes: a short dash, an "L" and a small ring, so it reads as a playful "-Lo". The full logo pairs the circle with a rounded, geometric "Lovart" wordmark. Both files come from the logo in the header of lovart.ai.

The logo is a single near-black color (#100F09) and has no accent color. It disappears on dark backgrounds, so use the monochrome version in dark themes.

## When to use this logo

Use the Lovart logo in AI design tool comparisons, creative agent roundups, marketing and brand-design workflow diagrams, and lists of generative design apps. The circular mark suits compact spots such as app grids, avatars and provider pickers, while the full logo fits landing pages, partner sections and tool directories. It pairs naturally with Canva, Figma, Midjourney, Krea and Ideogram.
