## About the Volcano Engine logo

The Volcano Engine mark is a row of five pointed peaks, one tall and several smaller ones, layered like a mountain range. The full logo pairs the peaks with the Chinese wordmark 火山引擎 ("volcano engine"). These files match the logo in the header of volcengine.com.

The peaks alternate between blue (#006AFF) and cyan (#00DCFF), and the wordmark is a dark slate (#1C2633). The site switches to a white wordmark on dark backgrounds, so in dark themes use the mark alone or the monochrome version.

## When to use this logo

Use the Volcano Engine logo in cloud provider comparisons, LLM provider pickers and model gateways that route to Doubao models through Volcano Ark, and in architecture diagrams for apps deployed on ByteDance's cloud. It pairs naturally with ByteDance and Doubao, and with Alibaba Cloud, Tencent Cloud, Huawei Cloud and Baidu AI Cloud in overviews of Chinese cloud platforms.
