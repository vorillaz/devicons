## About the Hailuo AI logo

The Hailuo AI mark is a spiral shell drawn inside a rounded shape with one square corner. It is filled with a diagonal gradient that runs from yellow (#FFD400) through orange (#FF891F), pink (#E4659A) and magenta (#DB2ED7) to violet (#7264F4). The lockup on hailuoai.video adds a "Hailuo AI" wordmark, near-black (#15171F) in light mode.

The gradient mark works on light and dark backgrounds. The wordmark switches to white in the site's dark theme, so use the monochrome lockup there.

## When to use this logo

Use the Hailuo AI logo in AI video generator comparisons, text-to-video and image-to-video tool roundups, creator tool directories and pipelines that render clips with MiniMax video models. It pairs naturally with MiniMax, and with other video generators such as Runway, Pika, Kling, Luma Dream Machine and Google Veo.
