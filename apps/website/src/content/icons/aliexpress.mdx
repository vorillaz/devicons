## About the AliExpress logo

The AliExpress icon is a shopping bag: an orange (#FF9900) band across the top of a red (#E62E04) bag, with a white smile-shaped handle. The wordmark spells "AliExpress" with "Ali" in orange (#F7971D) and "Express" in red (#E43225), with a small four-pointed sparkle above the "i".

Both versions read well on light and dark backgrounds.

## When to use this logo

Use AliExpress in dropshipping and e-commerce tool docs, marketplace comparisons, affiliate integrations and shopping app designs. It pairs naturally with Alibaba Cloud, Amazon, eBay and Shopify. Use the bag icon for app grids and compact slots.
