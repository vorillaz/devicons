## About the Manus logo

The Manus glyph is a hand-drawn hand mid-gesture, with three short strokes radiating above it. The name is Latin for "hand", taken from MIT's motto "Mens et Manus" (mind and hand). The primary logo pairs the glyph with a lowercase serif "manus" wordmark, and the brand page also offers the glyph on its own and as a rounded-square app icon.

The palette is deliberately minimal: Black #34322D, Gray #F8F8F8 and White #FFFFFF. The guidelines forbid recoloring, rotating, flipping, outlining or filling the logo, and ask that it stay legible against its background. On dark surfaces, use the white (mono) version.

## When to use this logo

Use the Manus logo in AI agent comparison tables, agentic workflow diagrams, roundups of autonomous research and browser-automation tools, and integration docs such as Slack connectors. It pairs naturally with OpenAI's ChatGPT agent, Claude, Genspark and Devin. The glyph works as a compact badge in tool pickers. Use the lockup when the name needs to read at a glance.
