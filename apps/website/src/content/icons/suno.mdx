## About the Suno logo

The Suno logo is a bold, wide "SUNO" wordmark. Its letters look partly melted: the right stem of the U and the diagonal of the N bend in wavy, liquid curves. There is no separate symbol. The app icon places the same wordmark in white on an orange-to-pink circle, and suno.com uses the wordmark alone in the site header. This file comes from the vector logo on suno.com.

The wordmark is a single color. It is black here and white on Suno's dark website. The black version disappears on dark backgrounds, so use the monochrome version in dark themes.

## When to use this logo

Use the Suno logo in AI music tool comparisons, generative audio landscape maps, creator-tool directories, and docs or tutorials about making songs with Suno. It pairs naturally with Udio, ElevenLabs and Stable Audio in audio AI lineups, and with Pika, Luma and Runway when you show a wider generative media stack.
