## About the Fish Audio logo

The Fish Audio mark is a row of rounded vertical bars, like an audio waveform, whose outline forms a fish. The bars that make up the lower fin are a lighter grey. The full logo sets the mark next to "Fish Audio" in a semibold geometric sans-serif. The mark is the vector logo from the fish.audio header, and the lockup follows the logo used in the Fish Audio docs.

The main bars are black (#000000) and the fin bars are grey (#7A7A7A). For dark backgrounds the docs switch to white with a lighter grey (#858585), so use the monochrome version in dark themes.

## When to use this logo

Use the Fish Audio logo in text-to-speech provider pickers, voice cloning comparisons, voice agent architecture diagrams and docs for SDK or API integrations. It also fits lists of open speech models that include Fish Speech. Natural pairings are ElevenLabs, Coqui, OpenAI and Hugging Face, along with LiveKit for real-time voice apps.
