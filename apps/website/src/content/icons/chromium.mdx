## About the Chromium logo

The Chromium logo has the same shape as the Chrome logo: a circle split into three segments around a central dot. Instead of Chrome's red, yellow and green, Chromium uses shades of blue, from deep blue (#1972E7) and Google blue (#4285F4) to pale blue (#AFCCFB). A white ring separates the centre from the outer segments.

The blue logo reads well on light and dark backgrounds.

## When to use this logo

Use Chromium in browser engine comparisons, Electron and headless browser docs, Puppeteer or Playwright setup guides and web compatibility tables. It pairs naturally with Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, Firefox and Electron.
