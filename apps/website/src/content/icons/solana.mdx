## About the Solana logo

The Solana logo mark is three slanted bars stacked like a stylised "S", with the middle bar slanting the opposite way. The bars are filled with Solana's gradient from purple (#DC1FFF) to green (#00FFA3).

The gradient reads well on light and dark backgrounds.

## When to use this logo

Use Solana in blockchain comparisons, wallet and dApp integration docs and web3 stack diagrams. It pairs naturally with Ethereum, Rust, Tether, Coinbase and MetaMask.
