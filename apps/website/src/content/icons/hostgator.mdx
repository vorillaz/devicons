## About the HostGator logo

The HostGator logo is Snappy, the company's alligator mascot: a blue (#2E93EE) cartoon gator with a yellow (#FFCF00) belly and an orange mouth, standing upright with a hand on its hip. This version shows the mascot without the "HostGator" wordmark.

Snappy reads well on light and dark backgrounds.

## When to use this logo

Use HostGator in web hosting comparisons, WordPress deployment guides, domain and DNS setup tutorials and agency stack overviews. It pairs naturally with GoDaddy, Namecheap, cPanel, WordPress and Cloudflare.
