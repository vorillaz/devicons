## About the Bilibili logo

Bilibili's primary logo is the hand-drawn, lowercase "bilibili" wordmark, which Wikipedia lists as in use since 2009. Its slanted, uneven strokes and triangular counters give it a doodled, playful feel that suits the platform's anime and gaming roots. The separate TV-set glyph, a rounded screen with two antennae and two eyes, is used across bilibili.com as the home icon and ships here as the standalone mark.

The wordmark uses Bilibili's brand blue (#00AEEC) and the TV glyph its brand pink (#FF6699). Both values come from bilibili.com's stylesheet, and both hold up on light and dark backgrounds.

## When to use this logo

Use the Bilibili logo in creator platform comparisons, social sharing button rows for Chinese audiences, video embed docs and marketing pages that list where content is published. It pairs naturally with YouTube, Douyin, Weibo and Xiaohongshu. The TV glyph is the better pick for compact share buttons. Use the wordmark when people need to recognize the brand by name.
