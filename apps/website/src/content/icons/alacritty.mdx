## About the Alacritty logo

The Alacritty logo is a tall, pointed "A" in an orange-to-yellow gradient (#EC2802 to #FCB200), with a blue (#069EFE) diamond hanging below its crossbar like rocket exhaust.

The logo reads well on light and dark backgrounds.

## When to use this logo

Use Alacritty in terminal emulator comparisons, Rust project showcases and dotfiles READMEs. It pairs naturally with tmux, Neovim, Rust and Ghostty.
