## About the Airbnb logo

The Airbnb symbol is the Bélo, a single looping line that combines a location pin, a heart and an upside-down "A". Airbnb introduced it in its 2014 rebrand. The symbol and the lowercase "airbnb" wordmark use the brand's coral red (#FF5A5F), which Airbnb calls Rausch. The lockup sets the Bélo next to the wordmark.

The coral red reads well on light and dark backgrounds.

## When to use this logo

Use Airbnb in travel app comparisons, vacation rental and property management tooling, booking flow designs and partner logo rows. It pairs naturally with Uber, Google Maps, Stripe and Booking.com. Use the Bélo on its own for app grids and small UI slots.
