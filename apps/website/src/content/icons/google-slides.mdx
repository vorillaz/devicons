## About the Google Slides logo

The Google Slides icon is a yellow page (#F4B400) with a folded top-right corner in pale yellow (#FADA80) and a white outlined rectangle, a slide, in the middle. It shares its folded-page shape with the Google Docs and Google Sheets icons.

The icon reads well on light and dark backgrounds.

## When to use this logo

Use Google Slides in presentation tool comparisons, Google Workspace integration docs, export-to-slides buttons and docs for the Google Slides API. It pairs naturally with Google Sheets, Microsoft PowerPoint, Canva and Keynote.
