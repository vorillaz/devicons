## About the Warp logo

The Warp logo is two overlapping, slanted rounded shapes on a black rounded square: a dark blue one (#006CA7) behind and a bright cyan one (#00CBE4) in front.

The black tile blends into dark backgrounds, but the blue shapes stay visible.

## When to use this logo

Use Warp in terminal comparisons, AI coding tool roundups and developer setup guides. It pairs naturally with Ghostty, iTerm2, Alacritty, Tabby and Claude Code.
