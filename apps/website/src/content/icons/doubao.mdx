## About the Doubao logo

The Doubao mark is a rounded, play-button-like shape made of interlocking ribbons in violet, blue and mint around an open center. It identifies the Doubao large model family (豆包大模型) that ByteDance offers through its Volcano Engine cloud. The full logo pairs the mark with the Chinese wordmark 豆包大模型. The consumer Doubao chatbot, launched in August 2023, uses an illustrated avatar as its web icon instead.

The ribbons are violet #A569FF, blue #1E37FC and mint #37E1BE, and the wordmark is black. The black wordmark disappears on dark backgrounds, so use the monochrome version or the mark alone in dark themes.

## When to use this logo

Use the Doubao logo in model comparison tables, LLM provider and model pickers, benchmark charts, and docs for SDKs or gateways that call Doubao models through Volcano Engine. It pairs naturally with Qwen, DeepSeek, Kimi, ERNIE and GLM in overviews of Chinese AI models, and with ByteDance when you show the company behind them.
