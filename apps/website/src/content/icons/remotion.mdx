## About the Remotion logo

The Remotion logo is a blue (#0B84F3) play button with rounded corners. It sits inside two larger copies of the same shape at lower opacity, which gives it a soft, layered glow.

The blue logo reads well on light and dark backgrounds.

## When to use this logo

Use Remotion in video generation tool comparisons, React ecosystem roundups and programmatic video tutorials. It pairs naturally with React, Next.js, FFmpeg and OBS Studio.
