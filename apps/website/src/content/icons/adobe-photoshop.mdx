## About the Adobe Photoshop logo

The Photoshop icon is a dark navy rounded square (#001E36) with the letters "Ps" in bright blue (#31A8FF). It follows Adobe's app icon system, where each Creative Cloud app gets a two-letter mnemonic on a dark tile in its own colour. The rounded tile dates from Adobe's 2020 icon refresh.

The navy tile blends into very dark backgrounds, but the blue letters stay legible.

## When to use this logo

Use Adobe Photoshop in creative tool comparisons, design workflow diagrams, .psd file-type icons and docs for Photoshop plugins or the Photoshop API. It pairs naturally with Adobe Illustrator, Adobe Firefly, Figma, GIMP and Canva.
