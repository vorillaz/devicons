## About the Mercado Libre logo

The Mercado Libre icon is a bright yellow (#FFE600) oval with a navy (#2D3277) outline and two white hands shaking in the middle. The lockup adds "mercado libre" in rounded navy lowercase letters next to the handshake. In Brazil the same logo reads "mercado livre".

The yellow oval reads well on light and dark backgrounds. The navy wordmark is hard to read on dark surfaces, so use the icon or the monochrome lockup there.

## When to use this logo

Use Mercado Libre in Latin American e-commerce integrations, multichannel seller tools, marketplace comparisons and docs for the Mercado Libre or Mercado Pago APIs. It pairs naturally with Shopify, eBay, AliExpress, PayPal and Stripe.
