## About the Tether logo

The Tether logo is a teal-green (#50AF95) shape like a cut gemstone, wide at the top and pointed at the bottom. Inside it is a white "T" with a flat ellipse wrapped around its stem, the ₮ symbol Tether uses for USDT.

The teal logo reads well on light and dark backgrounds.

## When to use this logo

Use Tether in crypto payment integrations, stablecoin and DeFi dashboards, exchange listings and wallet asset pickers. It pairs naturally with Binance, Coinbase, Ethereum, Solana and MetaMask.
