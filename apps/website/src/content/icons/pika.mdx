## About the Pika logo

The Pika mark is a compact, rounded animal silhouette with a swept-back ear, cut by two thin curved notches. The wordmark is a wide geometric "Pika" with a dotted i and a single-story a. On pika.art the two appear separately: the wordmark sits in the header and the mark in the footer. Both files come from the vector SVGs on pika.art.

Both are single-color. The color files are black, and the site shows them in white on dark sections. The black versions disappear on dark backgrounds, so use the monochrome versions in dark themes.

## When to use this logo

Use the Pika logo in AI video generator comparisons, generative media tool directories, creator-economy stack diagrams, and docs or tutorials about making or editing clips with Pika. Use the mark alone for avatars, model pickers and small badges. It pairs naturally with Runway, Luma, Kling, Google Veo and OpenAI Sora.
