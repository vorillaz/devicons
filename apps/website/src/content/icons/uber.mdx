## About the Uber logo

The Uber logo is the word "Uber" in black, set in Uber Move, the company's custom typeface. Uber introduced this wordmark in its 2018 rebrand. It replaced the 2016 logo built around the "bit" symbol with a simpler, more legible design.

The black wordmark disappears on dark surfaces, so use the monochrome version in white there.

## When to use this logo

Use Uber in ride-hailing and mobility app comparisons, travel and expense integrations, delivery and logistics tooling and partner logo rows. It pairs naturally with Airbnb, Google Maps, Stripe and DoorDash.
